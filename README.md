# Theme Hospital — Custom Mod Edition

A custom mod for **Theme Hospital** (built on CorsixTH v0.70.3) adding custom hireable staff, two brand-new clinic rooms with custom machines and diseases, and authentic 1997-style pixel-art animations — all unlocked right from **Level 1**.

## Quick Download & Play (Windows)

1. Download **`ThemeHospital-CustomMod-Windows.zip`** from the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/cjmiddleton225/theme-hospital-custom-mod/releases/latest)**.
2. Extract the full folder anywhere on your PC (do not run from inside the ZIP preview).
3. Double-click **`CorsixTH.exe`**.
4. On first launch, select your original 1997 **Theme Hospital** installation folder (e.g., GOG.com, EA App, or original CD) when prompted.
5. Start a new game on **Level 1** — all custom staff, rooms, and diseases are available immediately!

## What's in the Mod

### Custom Hireable Staff (Guaranteed on Level 1)
- **Ella (Doctor)** — *"Very proficient and hard working, but can't keep her bedroom tidy."*
  - High skill, strong stamina, and high attention to detail, with a periodic low-frequency quirk of dropping a bit of litter on the floor.
- **Dinkles (Receptionist)** — *"80% Lovely, 20% Doesn't listen, 100% Cute."*
  - Standard receptionist stats with a playful quirk of occasionally meandering briefly when relocated before heading back to reception duties.

### Custom Clinics, Machines & Diseases (Unlocked on Level 1)
- **Quoiging Room & Quoiging Chair — Cures *Whingers***
  - A custom 1997-style medical recliner fitted with a pressurized giggle-tank, analog Giggle-O-Meter gauge, and feather holder.
  - During treatment, the Doctor puts on a spinning jester-propeller silly hat while oversized red tickling gloves and feathers tickle the patient until they laugh themselves better.
- **Up-Down Room & Up-Down Rig — Cures *Non-Listeners***
  - A custom clinic gantry crane with a control console, height gauge, and floor target pad.
  - During treatment, a padded crimson ankle clamp lowers onto the patient's leg, hoists them off the floor, and dangles them upside down until they start listening.

## Customizing the Art

All 24 custom sprite frames live in [`CorsixTH/Bitmap/mod_sprites/`](CorsixTH/Bitmap/mod_sprites/) and are generated by [`tools/generate_mod_sprites.py`](tools/generate_mod_sprites.py) using a 2:1 isometric 3D-shaded pixel-art renderer quantized to Theme Hospital's 1997 palette.

To swap in your own artwork, replace any PNG in `CorsixTH/Bitmap/mod_sprites/` and recompile the sprite sheet:

```bash
python tools/generate_mod_sprites.py --compile-only
```
